# Recommendations for Whoop (Google Play reviews)

## Fix: Subscription & price
- Evidence: Mentioned in 33% of negative reviews; a mention lowers the rating by 1.18 stars on average.
- Action: Make the membership's value visible: monthly 'what your data did for you' recaps, clear plan comparisons, and a save offer or pause option in the cancel flow.
- Target: Cut the share of negative reviews mentioning subscription & price from 33% to 25%.

## Fix: App bugs & updates
- Evidence: Mentioned in 24% of negative reviews; a mention lowers the rating by 1.25 stars on average.
- Action: Add a release-quality gate: staged rollouts, crash-rate alerts, and a fast hotfix path for versions whose ratings dip after launch.
- Target: Cut the share of negative reviews mentioning app bugs & updates from 24% to 18%.

## Fix: Customer service
- Evidence: Mentioned in 17% of negative reviews; a mention lowers the rating by 1.07 stars on average.
- Action: Set and publish a support response-time target, and route reviews that mention support to a recovery team.
- Target: Cut the share of negative reviews mentioning customer service from 17% to 13%.

## Amplify: Strain, recovery & HRV
- Evidence: Mentioned in 9% of positive reviews; a mention raises the rating by 0.29 stars on average.
- Action: Keep investing in the core strain and recovery experience; it is a differentiator.
- Target: Grow the share of positive reviews mentioning strain, recovery & hrv.

## Win switchers: Battery & charging
- Evidence: Oura reviewers complain about battery & charging far more often (17% vs 3% of negative reviews).
- Action: Use battery & charging as a proof point in comparison marketing and switcher offers aimed at Oura users.
- Target: Track reviews mentioning Oura and the rating among them.
